# Workflow prompts

Examples of requests, not completed customer results. Start with a small batch and inspect the data before scheduling outreach.

## Recruiting

```text
Find up to 10 senior backend engineers in the United States with Python
and distributed systems experience. Show available work history and
education. Save suitable profiles to a project named Backend hiring.
Respect my search and contact credit limits. Do not send email.
```

## Personalized outreach

```text
Review the saved candidate's experience. Draft a short email explaining
why this role fits their background. Show me the recipient, draft,
timezone and proposed Tuesday 9am send time before scheduling.
```

## Sales prospecting

```text
Find up to 10 engineering leaders at US developer tools companies.
Explain the fit using available profile information and save the matches
to a sales project. Do not invent missing email addresses.
```

## Recurring sourcing

```text
Help me configure a daily sourcing campaign for up to 10 new matching
profiles. Avoid duplicates, stay within my plan and stop at 100 profiles.
Show the filters, schedule, timezone and budget before activating it.
Keep outreach disabled until I configure an email sequence.
```

Your assistant's available tools and permissions determine what it can execute. Review schedules and active workflows in Graviola. External messages require an authorized mailbox and applicable access.
